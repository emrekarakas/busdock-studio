# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in BusDock Studio a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and prioritize community safety. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for behavior they consider inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact channel listed in the repository security policy. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
